Free Year 3 maths worksheets

Year 3 is the start of Key Stage 2. Children read and write numbers to 1000, add and subtract three-digit numbers with the formal column method, learn the 3, 4 and 8 times tables, count in tenths and compare fractions, and tell the time including Roman numerals. These printable worksheets give unlimited practice on each objective.

Tips for parents

  • Learn the 4 and 8 tables by doubling the 2 and 4 tables.
  • Always line up digits carefully in column methods — squared paper helps.
  • Estimate before calculating to spot silly mistakes.

Frequently asked questions

What is taught in Year 3 maths?

Numbers to 1000, formal written addition and subtraction, the 3, 4 and 8 tables, fractions and time.

Is there a Year 3 times tables test?

No, the national multiplication tables check is in Year 4, but Year 3 is when the 3, 4 and 8 tables are learned.

Do the sheets include answers?

Yes, turn on the answer key before printing.
