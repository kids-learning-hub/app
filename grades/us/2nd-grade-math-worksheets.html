They also meet arrays, the picture behind multiplication. These sheets practise each of those skills and give a fresh printable page every time you click.

Tips for parents

  • Use real coins to make amounts before working on paper.
  • Practise counting by 5s with a clock face and by 10s with a hundred chart.
  • Ask your child to explain how they solved a sum, not just the answer.

Frequently asked questions

What should a 2nd grader know in math?

Adding and subtracting within 100, place value to 1000, telling time to 5 minutes, solving money problems and the basics of arrays.

When do children start the times tables?

Arrays and skip counting in 2nd grade prepare for multiplication, which is the main focus of 3rd grade.

Can I print the same sheet for a whole class?

Yes — each sheet has a number; reopen it with the same number to print identical copies.
