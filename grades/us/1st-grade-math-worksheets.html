Free 1st grade math worksheets

In first grade children learn to add and subtract within 20 and become fluent within 10, understand that two-digit numbers are made of tens and ones, compare numbers to 100, and tell time to the hour and half hour. These free worksheets cover each skill with a new randomised sheet at every click, so you can practise the same skill several days in a row without repeating the same sums.

Tips for parents

  • Practise “make ten” facts until they are automatic: 7 + 3, 6 + 4…
  • Show tens and ones with bundles of 10 straws or sticks.
  • Read the clock together at real moments of the day.

Frequently asked questions

What should a 1st grader know in math?

Most first graders add and subtract within 20, understand tens and ones, count to 120 and tell time to the hour and half hour.

My child still counts on fingers. Is that OK?

Yes. Fluency within 10 develops during the year; strategies like making ten gradually replace finger counting.

Do the worksheets have answers?

Yes, switch on the answer key before printing.
