1. Who we are

This website, Kids Learning Hub, is operated by VS MEDIA. It presents our family of free educational mobile applications for children aged 4–12 and links to them on Google Play. For any question about this policy, contact us at vs.media.ste@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

This is a promotional website and it is designed to collect as little data as possible.

  • Newsletter (optional). If you choose to submit your email address in the newsletter form, it is used only to send you learning resources and app updates. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the site running.
  • Advertising & analytics cookies may be set by third parties as described in the next section.

Your account and your data

  • No account is required. Everything on the site — games, worksheets, stories — works without signing in.
  • Local preferences and progress. Your chosen language and light/dark theme, the stars and badges earned in the games, and the pages you save as favourites are stored in your browser's localStorage on your own device. Unless you sign in to the optional parent account described below, this never leaves your device and is not sent to us.
  • Optional parent account. A parent or teacher (an adult) may choose to create a free account in “My space”, with a Google account or an email address and password. We then store, for that account only: the email address and sign-in method, the stars and badges earned on the site, the list of favourite pages, the interface language and the date of the last update. These are used to keep stars and favourites the same on all of the family's devices. If you choose to fill in “My information”, we also store your own name, phone number, country and whether you are a parent or teacher, together with your email address. We use your phone number only to contact you by phone or WhatsApp about your account, the apps and offers, and only if you tick the box allowing it. You can change or remove these details at any time. We never ask for a child's name, photo, date of birth or email, and children do not sign in. Accounts are provided through Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which processes this data on our behalf on Google's servers; passwords are handled by Firebase and are never visible to us. This data is not used for online advertising and is not sold or shared. You can erase the account and everything stored with it at any time with the “Delete my account” button in “My space”, or by writing to us.
