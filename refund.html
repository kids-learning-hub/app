Refund Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

14-day money-back guarantee

If you are not happy with a purchase, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of the payment — no questions asked. This applies to every plan: monthly, yearly, lifetime, the Kids Learning Hub Pass and School Pass licences.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email vs.media.ste@gmail.com with the email address used for the purchase (and the order number if you have it).
  2. We confirm within 2 business days.
  3. The refund goes back to your original payment method through our payment partner, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

When a refund is made, the related Premium code is deactivated.

Subscription renewals

  • You can cancel a subscription at any time from the link in your receipt email; you will not be charged again.
  • If a yearly renewal was charged and you forgot to cancel, write to us within 14 days of the renewal and we will refund it in full.
  • Monthly renewals are not refunded after 14 days, but cancelling stops all future payments.

Technical problems

If a code does not work and we cannot fix the problem, we refund you in full, at any time.

School licences

Schools paying by invoice and bank transfer are refunded by bank transfer to the same account within 14 days of the payment.

Before a chargeback

Please contact us first — we answer quickly and refunds through us are faster than a bank dispute.
