Free Year 1 maths worksheets

Tips for parents

  • Practise number bonds to 10 until they are instant.
  • Use a 100 square to count forwards and backwards.
  • Fold paper into halves and quarters before doing the sheet.

Frequently asked questions

What maths is taught in Year 1?

Counting to 100, number bonds within 20, adding and subtracting to 20, halves and quarters, and o’clock and half-past times.

How long should Year 1 practice last?

About 10 minutes a day is plenty at this age.

Are the sheets free?

Yes, free to print or save as PDF, no sign-up.
