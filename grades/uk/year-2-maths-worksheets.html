Free Year 2 maths worksheets

Year 2 completes Key Stage 1: children work with two-digit numbers, add and subtract mentally and on paper, recall the 2, 5 and 10 multiplication tables, find 1/3, 1/4, 2/4 and 3/4 of shapes and quantities, use pounds and pence and tell the time to five minutes. These sheets cover every topic and are ideal revision before the end of KS1.

Tips for parents

  • Chant the 2, 5 and 10 tables in the car or on the way to school.
  • Use real coins to make amounts in pence.
  • Ask “How did you work that out?” after each answer.

Frequently asked questions

Which times tables are learned in Year 2?

The 2, 5 and 10 times tables, including the related division facts.

Is there a printable money sheet in pounds?

Our money sheets use dirhams, euros and dollars; the counting skills are the same with pounds and pence.

Can I print the same sheet for a class?

Yes, reopen a sheet with its number to print identical copies.
