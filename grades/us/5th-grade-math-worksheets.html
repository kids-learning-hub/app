Free 5th grade math worksheets

Fifth grade focuses on fractions and decimals: adding and subtracting fractions with unlike denominators, multiplying fractions, working with decimals to thousandths, and understanding volume. Children also solve longer word problems and begin to use coordinates. Alongside our worksheets, the printable classroom games — maths bingo and “I have, who has?” — are a fun way to keep facts sharp.

Tips for parents

  • Link fractions, decimals and money: 0.25 = 1/4 = a quarter.
  • Ask for a drawing on every fraction problem — it prevents most mistakes.
  • Play a printable class game on Fridays to review facts.

Frequently asked questions

What should a 5th grader know in math?

Operations with fractions, decimals to thousandths, multi-digit multiplication and division, volume and the coordinate plane.

My child struggles with fractions. Where to start?

Go back to pictures: shaded circles and bars, then equivalent fractions, before adding unlike denominators.

Are there games for the classroom?

Yes — print our maths bingo, “I have, who has?” and sea battle for the whole class.
