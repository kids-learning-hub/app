Ziko the Turtle and the Big Mountain

🐢 Kids Learning Hub · 👶 Ages 4–8 · ⏱ 3 min

At the edge of the green valley stood a big, big mountain. Every evening, Ziko the little turtle looked up at it and dreamed: “One day, I will watch the sunrise from the top.”

The animals laughed. “You?” giggled the rabbit. “You need fast legs for that!” “You need wings!” added the crow.

Ziko just smiled and packed a leaf full of berries. “I don't need to be fast,” he said. “I just need to not stop.”

He walked all morning — ten little steps, then a rest. Ten little steps, then a rest. The rabbit raced past him, laughing loudly.

Halfway up, Ziko found the rabbit sitting under a rock, bored and tired. “The top is too far,” the rabbit yawned. “I'm going home.”

Ziko kept going. When his legs ached, he rested — but he never turned around. Step… step… step.

In the soft blue hour before morning, Ziko pulled himself onto the very top. The sun rose slowly, painting the sky orange and pink, and the whole valley sparkled below.

“I made it,” whispered Ziko, “not because I was fast — because I kept going.”

🌟 The moral: Small steps, taken every day, climb the biggest mountains.

Talk about the story

What was Ziko’s dream?

To watch the sunrise from the top of the big mountain.

Why did the rabbit give up halfway?

He got tired and bored and thought the top was too far.

What was Ziko’s secret for reaching the top?

He didn’t need to be fast — he just never stopped.
