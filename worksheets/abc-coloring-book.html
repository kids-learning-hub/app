An alphabet book is how most children first link a letter to a sound and to a word. This page turns it into a colouring book: for every letter there is a big outlined letter in capital and small form, a word that starts with it, a picture to colour and a short, easy sentence to read and copy. The letter is outlined in blue inside the word, so children see where it is. Print one letter per page, or four letters on one page for a quick review — and switch the language to get the French, Spanish (with Ñ) or Arabic alphabet, each with its own words and sentences.

🔤 Best for ages 3–7

How to get the most from these sheets

  • Say the letter’s sound, not only its name, while your child colours it.
  • Find the blue letter inside the word together.
  • Read the sentence aloud, pointing to each word, then copy it on the lines.
  • Print one letter a week and staple the pages into a home-made alphabet book.

Frequently asked questions

How do I choose the letter?

Use the letter menu or the ◀ ▶ buttons above the sheet.

Why do some words not start with the letter?

Very few children’s words start with X, so that page uses a word that contains it: fox. The letter is outlined in blue inside the word.

Does it print in black and white?

Yes — that is the idea. The letter, the word and the picture are outlines ready to colour.
