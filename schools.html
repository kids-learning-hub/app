🔑 How it works

  1. Choose your tier and pay online, or ask for an invoice (bank transfer).
  2. You receive ONE school code by email.
  3. Teachers and pupils enter it in any of the 10 apps: Settings → Premium code (web version or apps downloaded from this site).

The code works on a number of devices matching your tier (with a margin). It renews each school year.
