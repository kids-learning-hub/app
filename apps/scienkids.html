ScienKids — Learn science by playing

ScienKids answers the questions children actually ask — why is the sky blue, where does rain come from, how does my body work. Ten science topics, each with illustrated lessons, seven kinds of game, safe experiments to try at home and colouring pages. Entirely offline.

What's inside

🪐

Ten topics to explore

Space, animals, plants, water, physics, chemistry, the Earth, the human body, the five senses and dinosaurs.

🧪

Experiments to do at home

Step-by-step experiments using kitchen materials, each ending with the explanation of why it happened.

🎮

Seven kinds of game

Quiz, true-or-false, memory, matching, drag-and-drop, odd-one-out and puzzles — so the same topic is revised seven different ways.

🎨

Colouring pages

A quiet activity between lessons — colour the planets, the animals, the parts of a plant.

👩‍🔬

Meet the scientists

Short portraits of the people behind the discoveries, so science has faces and stories, not only facts.

🔒

Safe by design

No chat, no accounts, no personalised ads, and a parental gate in front of anything that leaves the app.

Frequently asked questions

What age is ScienKids for?

Children from about 4 to 12. Younger children use the pictures, the colouring and the simpler games; older ones read the lessons and try the experiments.

What science topics are covered?

Ten: space, animals, plants, water, physics, chemistry, the Earth, the human body, the senses and dinosaurs — each with its own lessons, games and facts.

Are the experiments safe?

They use ordinary household and kitchen materials and are written for children, but an adult should always be present — as the app itself reminds you.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Every lesson, game, experiment and colouring page is stored in the app and works offline.
