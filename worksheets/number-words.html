Writing numbers in words joins maths and spelling, and it is needed for real life: cheques, forms and word problems all use it. Each sheet mixes two kinds of question — write the number in words, and read the words to write the digits — so children both spell and understand the number names. Switch the language and the sheet is rebuilt in French, Spanish or Arabic, with the right rules for each one.

🔤 Best for ages 6–10

How to get the most from these sheets

  • Start with 0–20: these names must be learnt by heart, since they do not follow a rule.
  • From 21, show the rule: tens word, hyphen, units word (forty-seven).
  • Say each number aloud before writing it: spelling follows the spoken name.
  • Keep a small list of the tens words (twenty, thirty, forty…) next to the sheet.

Frequently asked questions

Do you write “and” in English numbers?

The sheets use the American style, “three hundred forty-five”. In the UK “three hundred and forty-five” is also correct.

Which French spelling is used?

The traditional one: vingt et un, quatre-vingts, deux cents, with hyphens only below 100.

How are Arabic numbers written?

In the counting form taught at school: the units come before the tens (خمسة وأربعون), with مئة and مئتان for the hundreds.
