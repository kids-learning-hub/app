A word search looks like a simple pastime, but it quietly trains the skills behind fluent reading: recognising letter patterns, scanning carefully line by line and keeping a word in mind while you look for it. Each puzzle here is built around one theme, so children meet a family of related words, and you can switch the language to practise vocabulary in English, French, Spanish or Arabic.

🔎 Best for ages 5–12

How to get the most from these sheets

  • Start with the easy 8×8 level: words only go across and down.
  • Read the word list aloud together before starting, and talk about what each word means.
  • Teach a strategy: look for the first letter, then check its neighbours for the second letter.
  • Use the language switch to practise the same theme in a second language.

Frequently asked questions

Which directions are used?

Easy: across and down. Medium: diagonals too. Hard: some words are also written backwards.

Does it work in Arabic?

Yes. In Arabic the words read from right to left across the grid, and the letters are shown in their separate form.

Is there an answer key?

Yes, switch on the answer key before printing: the hidden words are highlighted.
