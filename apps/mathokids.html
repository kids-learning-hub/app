MathoKids — Learn primary maths by playing

MathoKids covers the whole primary maths programme — numbers, the four operations, times tables, mental arithmetic and word problems — as short exercises with instant feedback. Built by a mathematics teacher, it works entirely offline and keeps a record of every mistake so the child can come back to it.

What's inside

The whole primary programme

Numbers, addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, fractions and geometry — organised in modules that follow the school year.

A dedicated mental-arithmetic trainer and a times-table module that builds speed a few minutes at a time.

Frequently asked questions

What age is MathoKids for?

It follows the primary-school maths programme, so it suits children roughly 6 to 12. Younger children can start with the numbers and counting modules, which need no reading.

Does MathoKids work offline?

Yes, completely. Lessons, exercises, word problems and games are all stored in the app — no connection is needed at any point.

Who made MathoKids?

It is built by Ayyoub EL HAJJI, a mathematics teacher from Morocco. The exercise order, the calculation rules and the mistake-review system come from classroom experience, not from a generic template.

Is MathoKids free?

Yes, it is free to download and the core content is free to use. An optional paid unlock removes ads and opens the remaining modules.
