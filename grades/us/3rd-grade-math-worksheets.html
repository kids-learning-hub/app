Free 3rd grade math worksheets

Third grade is the year of multiplication and division. By the end of the year children are expected to know all products of two one-digit numbers from memory, understand division as sharing and grouping, and start fractions as numbers — halves, thirds, fourths, sixths and eighths. They also round to the nearest 10 and 100 and tell time to the minute. These free worksheets practise each skill with unlimited new sheets.

Tips for parents

  • Learn the tables in order: 2, 5, 10, then 3, 4, then 6, 7, 8, 9.
  • Five minutes of tables a day works better than an hour on Sunday.
  • Link division to multiplication: 24 ÷ 6 asks “6 times what is 24?”.

Frequently asked questions

What should a 3rd grader know in math?

Multiplication and division facts within 100, unit fractions, rounding to 10 and 100, area and time to the minute.

How do I help my child memorise the times tables?

Short daily practice, starting with the easy tables, plus games like our Times Tables Race and a poster on the wall.

Are the fractions worksheets visual?

Yes, they use shaded circles and bars so children see what each fraction means.
