Free Year 6 maths worksheets

Year 6 ends primary school with the KS2 SATs in May. Children use long multiplication and division, add and subtract fractions with different denominators, work with ratio and simple algebra, and solve multi-step problems. Quick, accurate arithmetic is worth many marks in the arithmetic paper, so regular short sheets on the basics are some of the best revision you can do.

Tips for parents

  • Do one short arithmetic sheet a day in the months before SATs.
  • Mark together and correct each mistake straight away.
  • Practise reading questions twice before answering.

Frequently asked questions

When are the Year 6 SATs?

The KS2 SATs take place in May, with papers in arithmetic, reasoning, reading and grammar.

What is the best way to revise maths?

Short daily practice on arithmetic and regular mixed word problems, with immediate correction.

Are these worksheets enough for SATs?

They cover core arithmetic; combine them with past papers for reasoning practice.
