Maths snakes & ladders — print and play

A new board every click, plus 24 challenge cards. You only need a die and a few tokens.

Snakes and ladders is often a child’s first board game, and it quietly teaches a lot: counting squares one by one, reading numbers to 100, and seeing that the board goes left to right, then right to left. Our version adds challenge cards on the ⭐ squares, so every game also becomes a few minutes of mental maths practice — without it ever feeling like homework.

🐍 Best for ages 5–10

How to get the most from these sheets

  • Print on thick paper or glue the board onto cardboard so it lasts. The cards can be reused many times.
  • Ask your child to count the squares aloud as they move their token: “23, 24, 25”. Little ones often count the square they are standing on — show them to start with the next one.
  • Choose the card level to match the child: addition for 5–6 year olds, subtraction around 7, times tables from 8.
  • Play together and make mistakes on purpose sometimes. Children love catching an adult who counted wrong — and they check much more carefully.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to play?

The printed board, the cut-out challenge cards, one die and a small token for each player — a coin, a button or a bottle cap works perfectly.

Is every board different?

Yes. Each click on “New game” places the snakes, ladders and ⭐ squares differently and makes 24 new challenge cards, so you can print a fresh game every time.

How long does a game last?

Usually 15 to 25 minutes with 2–4 players — just right for the attention span of young children.
