Follow your class in the 10 Kids Learning Hub apps. Free, no accounts, nothing stored on a server.

How to add pupils

  1. In the app, a parent opens Settings › Parents space › Share with the teacher.
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone camera: this page opens and the pupil is added.
  3. Or paste the links or codes received by WhatsApp or e-mail below (several at once is fine).
🔒 Data stays in this browser only.
