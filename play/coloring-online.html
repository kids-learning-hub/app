Online Coloring — Tap to Color

Choose a picture, pick a colour and tap to fill. Then print your masterpiece! For ages 3–8.

Why this game helps

Colouring builds hand–eye coordination, colour vocabulary and concentration. Tap-to-fill colouring lets even very young children enjoy the result; afterwards, print it, or try the paper colouring pages with crayons for fine motor practice.

🎨 About this game

Tap-to-fill colouring lets even the youngest children make a bright picture they are proud of. Eight drawings — a house, a fish, a rocket, a butterfly, a sunflower, an ice cream, a car and a turtle — plus a new mandala every time, sixteen colours, and a print button that prints only the picture. Talk about the colours while your child works (“what colour is the sky?”) to build vocabulary, and when the picture is finished, print it to hang on the fridge.

🎨 Best for ages 3–8

Tips for parents and teachers

  • Name each colour as your child picks it — in two languages if you like.
  • Ask for a rule: “all the windows the same colour”.
  • Print the finished picture and let your child sign it.
  • Then try the paper colouring pages with crayons for fine motor practice.

Frequently asked questions

Can I print the coloured picture?

Yes — the Print button prints only the picture, full page.

Can my child undo a colour?

Tap the shape again with another colour, or white to clear it; “Start again” clears the whole picture.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, it is made for touch screens as well as a mouse.
