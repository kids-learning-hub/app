🗣️ About this game

Linking a picture directly to a word is one of the fastest ways to build vocabulary in a new language — the brain stores “🍎 = apple” without passing through translation. Word Match lets children choose the language they are learning (English, French, Spanish, German or Arabic) and then match everyday pictures to the right word. It is perfect for a few minutes of practice before school or as a warm-up before a lesson.

🗣️ Best for ages 5–12

Tips for parents and teachers

  • Say each word aloud after choosing it — hearing and saying fixes it in memory.
  • Play the same language several days in a row before switching.
  • Point to the real objects at home and say their names in the new language.
  • For bilingual families, let your child “teach” you the words in the game.

Frequently asked questions

Which languages can my child learn?

English, French, Spanish, German and Arabic. Choose the language at the start of each game.

Does my child need to read?

A little. Pre-readers can play with an adult who reads the choices aloud; early readers practise reading at the same time.

Is there a full app for each language?

Yes — EngloKids, FrancoKids, SpanoKids, GermoKids and ArabiKids offer lessons, stories and dictation, free on Google Play.
