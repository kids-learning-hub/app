🇬🇧 United Kingdom · Year 4

Free Year 4 maths worksheets

Year 4 is the times tables year: in June, pupils in England take the statutory multiplication tables check, covering every table up to 12 × 12. Children also round to 10, 100 and 1000, use column addition and subtraction with four-digit numbers, recognise equivalent fractions and work with hundredths. Our sheets cover the tables to 10 × 10 with unlimited mixed practice, plus the other Year 4 objectives.

Tips for parents

  • Little and often: 5 minutes of mixed tables every day before the check.
  • Practise the 11 and 12 tables orally too — our sheets stop at 10.
  • Turn mistakes into a personal “tricky facts” list to review.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Year 4 multiplication tables check?

A short on-screen test taken in June in England, with 25 questions on tables up to 12 × 12 and 6 seconds per question.

Do your worksheets cover the 11 and 12 tables?

Our generators cover tables 2 to 10; practise 11 and 12 orally or with a poster.

What else is in Year 4 maths?

Four-digit numbers, rounding, column methods, equivalent fractions, decimals to hundredths and area.
