Free kindergarten math worksheets

Kindergarten math is about number sense: counting objects one by one, writing numbers to 20, comparing groups (“which has more?”), and first additions and subtractions within 10 using fingers and objects. Children also learn to name and describe flat and solid shapes. These printable sheets follow that path, from tracing digits to simple sums, and every click makes a brand-new A4 sheet so your child never runs out of practice.

Tips for parents

  • Count real things every day: stairs, spoons, cars on the street.
  • Use fingers freely — they are a natural first calculator.
  • Keep sessions to 10 minutes and stop while it is still fun.

Frequently asked questions

What math should a kindergartner know?

By the end of kindergarten most children count to 100 by ones and tens, write numbers to 20, compare groups and add or subtract within 10 using objects.

How many worksheets a day?

One sheet, or even half a sheet, is enough. Short daily practice beats long sessions.

Are these worksheets free?

Yes — free to print or save as PDF, with no sign-up.
